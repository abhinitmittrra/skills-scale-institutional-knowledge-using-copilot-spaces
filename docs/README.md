# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A landing page for OctoAcme's project management process documentation. These guides describe a consistent, iterative approach to taking work from an initial idea through delivery and continuous improvement.

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme's lifecycle begins with **initiation**: validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders and risks, and decide whether to proceed. **Planning** turns approved work into an actionable backlog with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies. During **execution**, teams track work on a project board, deliver in small increments, and use pull requests, regular reviews, and demos to maintain visibility.

The **Project Manager (PM)** coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications. The **Product Manager (PdM)** defines outcomes, prioritizes work, and measures success. Developers implement and test the solution, QA validates acceptance and quality, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use standups to raise progress and blockers, weekly delivery or PM syncs to review work and risks, and milestone demos and regular stakeholder updates to share progress. Blockers escalate from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery: new logic receives unit tests, integration tests are added where applicable, critical flows receive end-to-end smoke tests, and CI runs tests, linting, and security scans; manual QA is used when needed. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, retrospectives capture lessons and assign improvement actions with owners and due dates.
